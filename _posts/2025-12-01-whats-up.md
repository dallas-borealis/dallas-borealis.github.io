<p class="subtitle">1 Dec 2025 - Bellingham</p>

First: My general statement on AI: I will remain the human-in-the-loop, maintaining the critical thinking rather than the cognitive offloading that risks losing curiosity and creativity. We must be careful about the _deployment_ of these tools. As James Gurney put so well: ["I know! What we need is a machine can think of ideas and do art for us so we don't have to! Said no person ever."](https://www.instagram.com/p/DRr-9zDkeUu/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==)

Second: The Ray Bradbury Challenge-ish. Instead of the one poem, one essay, and one short story as Ray put it, I want to add a piece of music, complete an article/essay/etc., and draw every day. This is more that I think finding poems, essays, and short stories without spending a lot of money or exhausting my local library (or waiting for books to get shipped to my local branch) -- would be bad for the effort. It's about throwing things into my head for creative inspiration and not necessarily about being a better writer.

Third: Video games are okay. They, absolutely, can be the escapism a person needs. I really like them for the connection I am able to make with my kids (and their friends) that I didn't have when they were much younger. And from what I hear, they are okay with that.

That's all from Bellingham for now. Sees ya later.
